THE ARTIST

Meet Deb.

I’m a Santa Cruz jewelry artist drawn to the textures of weathered rock, the movement of water, and the clean lines of modern design.

Working with sterling silver, copper, brass, enamel, and stones, I make each piece by hand. I love the moment when a sheet of metal becomes something organic, personal, and entirely its own.

My work grows out of a lifelong connection to art, encouraged by my mother and grandfather. I’m a member of the Monterey Bay Metal Arts Guild and participate in Santa Cruz Open Studios.
